Physics plugins

Built by nezha_plugins:

LibraryRole
nezha_unified_pluginUnified hydrodynamics: blends the surface and Fossen models (PSC); <rigid_switching> gives PSC-RS.

nezha_wavefield_plugin, nezha_wavefieldmodel_pluginOcean wave field.
nezha_wave_probe_pluginWave-height probe.

nezha_terrain_detector_plugin, nezha_wheel_efficiency_pluginGround contact and wheel efficiency on soft or wet terrain.
